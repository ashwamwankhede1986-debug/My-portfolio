Projectile Launcher

Designed and engineered a precision-based hardware system to demonstrate the fundamental laws of motion. This project bridges theoretical physics with physical execution, allowing for real-time verification of trajectory paths.

Key Technical Features:

  • Variable Elevation: Calibrated launch angles from 0° to 90° for range testing.
  • Physics Logic: Used the range formula $R = \frac{v^2 \sin 2\theta}{g}$ to predict landing zones with high accuracy.
  • Force Optimization: Engineered a mechanical release mechanism to ensure consistent initial velocity across trials.

Developed as a core project at MIT Academy of Engineering, this build showcases the ability to translate complex equations into a functional physical device.
